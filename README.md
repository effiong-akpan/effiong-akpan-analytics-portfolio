# Effiong Akpan — Analytics Portfolio

This portfolio demonstrates my capability in **data analysis, operational intelligence, federal opportunity research, and decision-ready reporting**. Each project uses a clean structure, transparent assumptions, and professional deliverables designed for real-world business and operations teams.

All data in this repository is **synthetic or publicly available**. No confidential, proprietary, restricted, customer, partner, Sandia National Laboratories, or DOE BOOST information is included.

## 01 — Energy Operations Intelligence

A full analytical workflow demonstrating data cleaning and validation, KPI development, downtime analysis, value-loss estimation, dashboard creation, and executive decision reporting.

**Business question:** Which downtime categories and operational patterns contribute most to estimated production/value loss, and where should management investigate first?

**Planned deliverables:** synthetic dataset; data dictionary; cleaning notes; KPI calculations; dashboard screenshots; executive decision memo; and an analysis notebook.

## 02 — Federal Opportunity Decision Brief

A structured demonstration of requirements extraction, eligibility analysis, evidence-gap identification, timeline planning, and go/no-go decision support using publicly available information.

**Business question:** Should a hypothetical small energy-technology company pursue a public federal opportunity, and what evidence or partners are required before applying?

**Planned deliverables:** requirements matrix; evidence-gap analysis; deadline tracker; decision memo; sources list; and analysis notes.
