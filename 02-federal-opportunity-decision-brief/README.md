# Federal Opportunity Decision Brief

This project demonstrates structured federal-program analysis, requirements extraction, and decision-ready reporting using publicly available information.

## Objective

Evaluate a public federal funding opportunity for a hypothetical small energy-technology company and determine whether the opportunity should be pursued.

## Contents

- `sources/` — Public source links and citation notes
- `analysis/` — Requirement extraction, eligibility screen, evidence-gap map, and timeline
- `deliverables/` — Final opportunity decision brief

## Deliverables

- Eligibility matrix
- Requirements and compliance checklist
- Evidence-gap analysis
- Timeline and milestone tracker
- Go/no-go decision memo
- Recommended next steps

## Skills demonstrated

Technical research, requirements analysis, public-program interpretation, decision-memo writing, source documentation, and structured project planning.

## Disclaimer

All information will be publicly available. This project is illustrative and does not imply award likelihood, grant expertise, government endorsement, or affiliation with any agency.
